I will be supporting one candidate for the 2027 CSC 4-year PhD grant cycle. I'm currently looking for applicants. The profile of the prospective candidate should be as follows:
1. Have prior experience with geo-information science and/or remote sensing, or the ability to learn it quickly
2. Willing to work on a theme that is somewhat close to one of my on-going research themes:
    * Urban socio-economic outcomes (e.g. liveability)
    * Land use, especially novel & emerging land use characteristics
    * Urban green/blue infrastructure
    * Forests (not preferred at this time, lots ongoing already)
3. Comfortable working with one or more GeoAI-related methods (e.g. any direction within deep learning), or a strong willingness & aptitude to learn. 
4. Enjoy working with a variety of data modalities during the PhD (geodatasets, images, PoI data, text data, etc)

The topic will be designed in collaboration with the student based on their background and interests. Ideally I'd like to base part of the proposal on exploratory research for new data modalities.

If you are interested in applying then please reach out to me at a.h.levering \[at\] vu.nl with your CV, potential research idea, and other relevant information.
