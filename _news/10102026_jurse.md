Preparations for the 2027 edition of JURSE are well underway at the VU and ITC Twente. The call for papers has been out for a few weeks now, with the deadline set for the 17th of November. No fewer than 17 special topics have been proposed for this year's edition, so there'll be something for everyone. We also have details to share for the [social events](https://www.jurse2027.org/social-events/) at this time. The dinner will be hosted by the Tolhuistuin, and to get there we are taking one of the iconic canal tour boats. We'll share further updates when we have them, and we look forward to receiving you in Amsterdam next summer!
